---
title: Card skeletons
subtitle: How a dashboard looks while its cards and badges load
---

Each card and badge sits in an `ha-skeleton-overlay`, which reads a skeleton from what the card or badge draws: every name, state and value gets its own text skeleton, and icons, images, controls and drawings get their own pieces. When loading ends, each part fades in. Turn off **Loading** to see the real cards, or use **Simulate loading** to watch them arrive one by one.
